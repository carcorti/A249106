The specific family 4791*p^p is proved from the historical mechanism in the
manuscript. The targeted literature and related-entry searches did not establish
absolute priority. In particular, the historical Hoepli book referenced by the
sequence was not read; a negative search does not exclude an earlier statement.
The present package makes no stronger novelty claim.

`origins.tsv` records exact source hashes and every public copy/archive member.
`renames.json` records literal substitutions in the adapted validators and test
helpers. Scientific code in `src/` and the TeX source were copied without edits.
`data/witnesses.tsv` presents the same seven integer records as the local witness
table, with a header and tab separators. `data/family.tsv` is generated by
multiplying the proved six-coordinate seed by p^p at the eight displayed primes;
`validation/families.py` recomputes those chains directly.

The archive maps use workspace-relative historical names solely as provenance.
The public commands operate entirely from package-relative files or their own
temporary staging trees. They do not require the original workspace.
